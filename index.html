Privacy & credits

Your privacy, and who made what.

This site is a personal portfolio. It has no cookies, no analytics and no advertising, and it only receives your details if you choose to email me. It was also built with the help of AI tools, and you should know that up front.

Built with AI tools

In short: this portfolio was designed and built with the help of AI tools. The code, the layout and much of the written text were produced with AI assistance, mainly Anthropic’s Claude, and some of the sample visuals and videos may be AI-assisted or AI-generated.

I decide what goes live and I am responsible for what is published here. The AI tools helped me build and write faster; they did not replace my own judgement, and they are not a client, a colleague or an endorsement. Pieces that are concept samples are marked as samples on the skill pages.

Privacy notice

In short: no advertising, no analytics, no tracking pixels and no cookies. The only outside service the page loads is Google Fonts for the typefaces. Nothing you type here is stored on a server.

Who is responsible

Shubham Kangale, an independent designer and developer. You can reach me at shubhamkangale.info@gmail.com.

What this site stores on your device

Nothing. It sets no cookies and writes nothing to your browser’s local storage. Your light or dark mode choice only lasts until you reload the page.

Google Fonts

The typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. When the page opens, your browser asks Google’s servers for the font files, so Google receives technical details such as your IP address and browser type, under Google’s own privacy policy. I do not receive that information and the site does not use it for anything else.

When you contact me

The “Get in touch” form does not send anything to a server. When you press the button it opens your own email app with your name, email address and message filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send there. If you send it, the email travels through your email provider to my inbox (Gmail, run by Google). Writing to me directly by email works the same way.

I use what you send only to reply to you and to talk about your project, never for marketing. I keep it only as long as it is useful for our work together, and I will delete it if you ask.

The hosting provider

Like every website, this one is delivered by a hosting service, whose servers briefly process technical details such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse. That is governed by the host’s own privacy policy. I do not use those logs to identify visitors.

Links to other websites

The site links to my social profiles and to live project pages run by other people or companies. Once you follow a link you are on their site and under their privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask me what personal data I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it, by emailing me. I will reply within 30 days.
